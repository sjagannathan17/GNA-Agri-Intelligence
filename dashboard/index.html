Rainfall Impact on Risk
Key Finding

Zone IIa recorded a 19.9% rainfall deficit this season — the largest shortfall across all zones. Moisture stress during the critical Jan–Feb grain-fill period is a primary driver of its elevated risk score (0.52) and is factored into yield forecasts. Farmers in IIa who also lack inoculant face a compounding deficit of both water and nitrogen fixation.
